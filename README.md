## Axiom of Extensionality for Functions

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://latex.codecogs.com/svg.image?%5Ccolor%5Brgb%5D%7B0.176,0.831,0.749%7D%5Cforall%20f,g,%20%5Cmathrm%7Bdom%7D(f)=%5Cmathrm%7Bdom%7D(g)%20%5Cwedge%20%5Cforall%20x,%20f(x)=g(x)%20%5CRightarrow%20f=g" alt="Axiom of Extensionality for Functions" />

**The process is meaningless — the mapping is everything.**

**「万物皆函数 · 函数外延性公理」—— 过程毫无意义，映射代表一切**

You may write down a many-body Schrödinger equation or minimize a thermodynamic free energy. Yet if the input–output behavior is identical, the "profound" theory and an AI black box describe the world *equally well*. Science is just one — not necessarily optimal, not necessarily elegant — way of describing the world.
